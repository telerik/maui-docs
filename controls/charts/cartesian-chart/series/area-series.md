# .NET MAUI Cartesian Chart Area Series

The `AreaSeries` fills the area between the line that connects the data points and the axis.

## Features

The Area Series binds to data through the [common series properties]({% slug charts-cartesian-series %}) (`ItemsSource`, `HorizontalBinding`, and `VerticalBinding`).
